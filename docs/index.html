Clear Budget

A private desktop budget planner built around a question, not a ledger: will the month survive? It models forward solvency, bill timing and credit card pressure before problems arrive.

Download View source

Free and open source. Local-first. Multi-user. Your finances never leave your machine.

Most budgeting apps are retrospective ledgers that tell you where the money went. Clear Budget is forward-looking: it projects solvency for the months ahead and warns about mid-month overdrafts before they happen.

What Clear Budget does

Most budgeting software is retrospective. Clear Budget looks forward instead.

Safe to Spend Today

One actionable number at the top of the Solvency tab: the most you could spend today without pushing any day of the still-healthy forecast below your buffer, named with the day that constrains it. When the forecast goes under later regardless, that is a named warning beside the number, never summed into it. The buffer (twenty by default, zero if you plan to the wire) and the horizon (the whole forecast by default or only until your next income) are yours to set.

Forward solvency

Projects the next two months and answers the only operational question that matters: will the balance hold?

Mid-month overdraft alerts

Catches the dip when bills cluster before payday, even when the month still ends positive; it also names the first month you would go overdrawn.

Credit card pressure

Per-card utilisation, interest and a six-month balance projection, colour-coded by available headroom. Credit-limit changes can be scheduled ahead so every projection honours them.

Bill and income timing

Per-month skips, amount and due-day overrides, paid and received flags and one-off entries, plus end dates for bills and a delete that leaves earlier months untouched. A bill can also change amount from a month onward, for a rent increase, without rewriting what earlier months cost.

Self-maintaining balance

Set your bank balance once: dated bills and income are applied to it at local midnight on their day and ticked off automatically. Delete an applied item and the amount is handed back.

Graphs and shareable reports

See the month as a graph of the balance day by day or every card on one chart, step it month to month without leaving the graph and watch any overdrawn day paint in red. Export it as a single self-contained web page or export a projection across a range of months with a table that adds up: opening balance, income, bills, net, closing balance and the lowest point inside each month.

Private by design

Per-user isolated databases with a bcrypt sign-in and read-only viewer packages. A Remember me option keeps your credentials in the operating system's own credential store, never in a plain file. No cloud, no account servers. The one network request the app ever makes is a daily check of the project's GitHub releases for a newer version, carrying nothing about you or your budget; when one is published it offers the download for your platform. One click saves the whole budget to a single file you choose and loads it back later; the app remembers where.

Light and dark

One button switches the whole application, sign-in screen included and the choice is remembered. The graphs follow, with a palette chosen to stay readable on each background.

Usable without a mouse

An explicit keyboard path through every screen and dialog: Tab and the arrows step the same ring, the tab strip is walked tab by tab, long pages scroll from the keyboard and what has focus is always ringed.

25 currencies

Choose your currency once and every figure follows it. Defaults to GBP.

Installs without touching your data

A native package per platform: a per-user Windows setup needing no administrator rights, a macOS disk image and a Linux Flatpak. Installing, upgrading, repairing and uninstalling all deal in program files only, so your budgets, accounts and saved theme survive every one of them.

Four views, one question

Each tab is a different angle on whether the month (and the months ahead) holds together.

Monthly Budget

Bills and income for the month, with a balance that keeps itself up to date as dated items fall due, the projected month-end figure for future months and a mid-month overdraft warning.

Solvency

The Safe to Spend Today headline, overdraft status, a day-by-day forward projection for the next two months and per-card utilisation bars.

Credit Cards

One panel per card with live pro-rated balances and a six-month projection strip across every card.

Archive

Completed months summarised by year, with drill-down into any individual month.

Clear Budget is a free, private, local-first desktop budget planner for Windows, macOS and Linux. It leads with a Safe to Spend Today figure: the most you could spend today without any projected day across the whole forecast dropping below your chosen buffer, named with the day that constrains it. It models forward solvency for the next two months, detects mid-month overdrafts when bills cluster before income, tracks credit card utilisation and interest with a six-month balance projection and supports multiple users with bcrypt sign-in and isolated per-user databases. The month can be viewed as a graph and exported as a self-contained web page, along with a bank balance projection across any range of months. It has light and dark themes and a full keyboard path through every screen and dialog. Built with Python and PySide6, it runs offline with no cloud dependency (its only network request is a daily check of the project's GitHub releases for a newer version) and is open source under the LGPL-3.0 licence.
